# Errata

Report a correction with the [errata form](https://github.com/AlienMuse/alexander-grothendieck/issues/new?template=errata.yml). Please give the
PDF page number, the chapter and the exact place (paragraph, note number, figure).

Accepted corrections are collected here and incorporated into the next edition. Contributors of accepted
corrections are thanked by name in that edition, unless they ask not to be.

The released files are never changed in place: a corrected edition is published as a new version with its
own DOI, and earlier versions remain available.
